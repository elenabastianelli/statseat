Goals are confirmed by the next restart (a kickoff) or, with no restart and no VAR, disallowed-goal, foul or offside event before the final whistle, by the end of the period (v4.1); a crossing followed by another restart is a disallowed goal. Annotated goals include goals scored directly from a set piece (penalty, direct free kick: DFL nests the shot in the restart event); they are matched to the crossing at the restart's kick and stay out of the open-play shot checks and of xG. Clearances need at least 4 of the 7 conditions.

### What v4.2 changed, in short

- The kick belongs to the **nearest player** (or one within 0.3 m of the nearest on the ground, 0.5 m for aerial touches, because in an aerial duel the defender's tracked torso can be closer to the ball than the attacker's head). It removed most false "shots directed at a team-mate" (20 → 1) and "away from the goal" (9 → 3).
- A goal needs the ball to **enter between the posts**; a dead ball frozen by the provider is extrapolated for at most 0.08 s; with no restart, the end of the period confirms a goal unless a VAR, disallowed-goal, foul or offside event comes before the final whistle. A touch within 0.2 s of the crossing is not a deflection.
- **Woodwork** is measured on the ball's path between samples (including a bounce between two samples) and decided against the players' distances. A ball that has already crossed the goal line between the posts is in the net: what it hits there is not the woodwork and counts in no woodwork statistic.
- Annotations made during a stoppage are searched back up to 20 s; an aerial duel won with a shot may share the shot's frame. Both recovered header shots that v4 missed.
