### What the data can support (v4.2)

- **Distances and splits** per player over the whole time on the pitch, by ball in/out of play and in/out of possession, with the "≈" precision rule.
- **Top speeds** and other peak values, from valid measured samples only.
- **Ball speeds in live play** and event-anchored values from the synchronised frames.
- **Goals and saves** anchored on the physical outcome (highlights), and **calibration aggregates** for the synthetic generator: physical profiles by English role and the provider artefact profile.

### What it cannot support yet (v4.2)

- **Acceleration counts.** Effort thresholds are not adopted (section 8), and TRACAB acceleration accuracy is limited above about 4 m/s².

### Choices made in the v3 pass (documented, all in `config.py`)

- **Edges.** On top of ">= 25 identical samples beyond a line", an edge must be the outermost value on its side and come from >= 2 positions or runs. Otherwise frozen players behind the touchline would be read as edges (for example y = +35.87 m, 734 samples).
- **Swaps.** Labels are swapped back only when a mutual swap is followed by its mutual correction for the same pair (within 120 s). A single mutual swap is windowed only, because we cannot tell which side is wrong.
- **Jump causes.** Jumps are labelled by cause:
  - `swap`;
  - `substitution`;
  - `reentry`;
  - `crowding` (>= 3 players within 3 m);
  - `jump` (re-acquisition of a frozen or pinned track);
  - `unknown` (no signature; 55 episodes).

  All are excluded +-1 s.
- **Frozen runs.** A frozen run whose position is off the pitch and that resumes on the pitch is a `reentry`, invalid until 2 s after the resume. A frozen or pinned run that ends the track is an `exit`.
- **Event times.** The substitution event time is corrected by the period's event clock offset.
- **Kalman smoother.** The process noise (white jerk, 50 (m/s³)² s) is our choice.
- **New reason.** `missing` was added as an exclusion reason for samples with no position. None occur on J03WPY.

### What changed in the guidelines v3 update (2026-10-23)

- **Events located from trajectories** (section 9.3 steps 2-4): passes, shots and clearances at a ball touch (Savitzky-Golay |a| peak >= 8 m/s2, acting player within 2 m, ball then leaves him), duels at the minimum of the larger player-ball distance, order kept by a Needleman-Wunsch style alignment. Shots are confirmed by a goal (checked where the ball crosses the line), an opposing goalkeeper touch, or a ball heading into the goal frame; passes by a team-mate's next touch or open space. Numbers in section 4. The provider's own `CalculatedFrame` (tests only) agrees within 0.2 s for 23 of 24 shots.
