## Attributes 1-99

Our own scale, calibrated on DFL data. Attribute k of a player is the **k-th percentile among players of his position group** in `calibration/attribute_scales.json` (DFL open data, 6 matches, Bassek et al. 2025, CC-BY 4.0): a winger with pace 90 reaches about the DFL p90 top speed of wingers. Numbers are relative to the group, so players of different positions are compared through the physical value. `generator/insidethegame_gen/attributes.py` makes the numbers and holds the mapping (`physical_value`).

| Attribute | DFL quantity | Unit | Table used |
|---|---|---|---|
| pace | top speed: maximum of our valid speed per player-match | km/h | own group (GK, CB, FB, DM, AM, W, ST); CM: mean of DM and AM |
| acceleration | peak positive tangential acceleration on valid samples per player-match | m/s2 | own group (GK, CB, FB, DM, AM, W, ST); CM: mean of DM and AM |
| stamina | distance per 90 per player-match | m per 90 (measured time) | own group (GK, CB, FB, DM, AM, W, ST); CM: mean of DM and AM |
| work_rate | distance above 20 km/h per 90 per player-match | m per 90 (measured time) | own group (GK, CB, FB, DM, AM, W, ST); CM: mean of DM and AM |
| passing | pass success share (next touch by a team-mate) per player-match with >= 20 located passes | share 0-1 | own group (GK, CB, FB, DM, AM, W); ST: mean of AM and W; CM: mean of DM and AM |
| shot_power | peak valid ball speed in the 0.5 s after the kick of a confirmed open-play shot, headers excluded (per shot) | m/s | GK, CB, FB, DM, CM, AM, W, ST: overall |

How the numbers are made: a latent score from the player's quality, three shared factors (speed, endurance, technique) and noise, shifted by his main and second playing style, his strengths and an age curve, turned into a percentile with the normal distribution. A strength or a main style that names an attribute (for example "pace" or "pacy") guarantees a minimum value, and the validator checks it. There is no goalkeeper-specific or aerial attribute: the DFL calibration has no per-player aggregate for them.

## Money

Transfer fees are integers in euros (`fee_eur`). 131 transfers range from €250k to €15m (median €5.2m); loans, free transfers, returns from loan and academy promotions are 0. No salaries are generated.

## How the universe is made

- **Hand-written content** (`generator/insidethegame_gen/content/`): clubs, the stadium, staff, chairmen, crests, records, coach profiles, club history, legends, traditions, the roll of honour, the match programme and forecast, the building blocks of player profiles and the rules guide. Written with Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code; the team reviews it by hand.
- **Generated content** (seeded random generators): names, ages, birthplaces, careers and fees, the coaching history, fixtures and results, tables, the season simulation, line-ups, top scorers, team news, key duels and officials' numbers. These are generated because they must add up.
- **Calibrated on DFL aggregates**: player attributes 1-99 (section above), from `attribute_profile` (tier, quality, style), the strengths and the age, with one seeded stream per player; the scale is `calibration/attribute_scales.json`.
- **Not generated on purpose** (UNIVERSO-SPEC section 2, Could): club anthem lyrics, kiosk menus beyond the existing service list, lost property and Wi-Fi details, extra parking and timetable details, players' awards, and the results of other games played on the same matchday (Kit Man politely declines question 12).

## Limits

- The real-name check uses a list of famous people, clubs, stadiums and brands. It cannot cover every real person, so the team still reads the names once by hand.
- Career numbers before this season, other clubs' coaches and the referees' numbers are invented and only checked for plausibility.
- Other clubs have no squads: only their leading scorer is named, for the top-scorer table.
