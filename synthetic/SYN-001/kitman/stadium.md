# Lanternworks Stadium

## Key facts

- Home of Brackwater Athletic. Address: 1 Lantern Quay, Brackwater.
- Opened in 2004. Capacity 60,400. Record attendance 60,388 (Brackwater Athletic v Calderholm United, 2019/20).
- Pitch: 105 x 68 m, hybrid grass, with undersoil heating.
- Roof: covers every seat; the pitch is open. Floodlights: LED, installed 2021. Big screens: north-west corner, south-east corner.
- Built on the site of the Harrow Quay lamp factory where the club was founded. The four red-brick chimneys of the factory were kept and stand behind the North stand; at night they are lit amber.

## History: memorable matches here

- August 2004: the opening match, a 2-2 draw with Calderholm United in the Estuary Derby.
- August 2018: Brackwater beat Ravensgate City 2-1 to win the Meridian Super Shield, the first trophy lifted at the Lanternworks.
- 2019/20: the record attendance of 60,388, for the derby against Calderholm United.
- May 2022: the stadium hosted the Meridian Cup final, won by Hollin Vale United.

## Getting there

- Tram: Tram line 2 to the 'Lanternworks' stop, 300 m from Gate G. Extra trams run after the match.
- Train: Brackwater Harbour station, a 15-minute walk along the quay.
- Bus: Park-and-ride from the Brackwater Ring Road, every 10 minutes from 2 hours before kick-off.
- Bike: Free bike racks at Gates C and D.
- Ferry: The estuary ferry ('the derby boat') lands at Lantern Pier, next to Gate F, the away entrance.

## Stadium rules

- Gates open 90 minutes before kick-off.
- Bags larger than an A4 sheet are not allowed; there is no left-luggage service.
- The whole stadium, inside and outside the stands, is smoke-free.
- Glass, cans, flares and fireworks are not allowed.
- All kiosks are cashless: cards and phones only.
- Re-entry is not allowed once you leave the stadium.
- Children under 14 must be with an adult.
