### Clubs in the Meridian League

- **Alder Cross FC** (The Crossmen, Alder Cross, founded 1908; Crossways, 20,500; head coach Fiete Brenninkhof): A railway-junction club promoted two seasons ago and fighting to stay up.
- **Brackwater Athletic** (The Lamplighters, Brackwater, founded 1889; Lanternworks Stadium, 60,400; head coach Mara Ellingham): A harbour-town club founded by lamp-factory workers, famous for its loud North stand and a youth academy that feeds the first team.
- **Brindlemoor Athletic** (The Moorhens, Brindlemoor, founded 1906; Heathside Ground, 21,900; head coach Orrin Winmere): Back in the top division after a long absence, built on a thrifty scouting network.
- **Calderholm United** (The Kilnmen, Calderholm, founded 1894; The Kilnyard, 38,200; head coach Dario Castellane): Born in the pottery district across the estuary from Brackwater, Calderholm play a direct, high-pressing game and have won the league four times.
- **Copperhythe FC** (The Smiths, Copperhythe, founded 1912; The Forge, 18,700; head coach Gonçalo Quaresmeiro): One of the smallest grounds in the top division, and the loudest atmosphere per seat, according to its fans.
- **Ellesby Town** (The Bells, Ellesby, founded 1899; Belfry Road, 22,800; head coach Kieran Linworth): A cathedral-town club; the bells ring after every home win.
- **Fennmoor Town** (The Herons, Fennmoor, founded 1901; Reedbank Park, 24,100; head coach Benedict Penham): A marshland club known for patient passing and the heron painted on the roof of its main stand.
- **Glenvarra FC** (The Stags, Glenvarra, founded 1910; Highmoor Park, 19,800; head coach Julian Eastbrook): The highest ground in Meridia, where visiting teams complain about the wind.
- **Hollin Vale United** (The Valesmen, Hollin Vale, founded 1900; Hollin Park, 30,200; head coach Lennox Norlow): Twice cup winners in the last five years, known for a strong defence.
- **Kingsfold Rovers** (The Kingfishers, Kingsfold, founded 1892; Riverside Meadow, 29,400; head coach Linus Melwood): Riverside club with a famous academy and a habit of selling its best young players.
- **Lowmarsh FC** (The Reeds, Lowmarsh, founded 1920; Reedmoor, 17,400; head coach Darragh Cullinane): Newly promoted, playing in the top division for the first time in 31 years.
- **Marrowdale Wanderers** (The Drovers, Marrowdale, founded 1883; Drovers Field, 21,900; head coach Octavian Fairby): A valley club that once played on a cattle market; tangerine shirts and a reputation for late goals.
- **Ostwick Albion** (The Weavers, Ostwick, founded 1898; Loom Street, 33,700; head coach Aldous Goldbrook): A textile-town club from the capital's east side, proud of a stand built by its own supporters in 1951.
- **Port Ellery FC** (The Tidesmen, Port Ellery, founded 1887; Seawall Road, 31,500; head coach Cormac Norwell): The oldest club in Meridia, three-time champions, with a ground built right against the sea wall.
- **Ravensgate City** (The Ravens, Ravensgate, founded 1905; Gatehouse Arena, 45,800; head coach Callan Crandale): The capital's biggest club and current champions, with the largest budget in the league.
- **Saltrigg Rangers** (The Diggers, Saltrigg, founded 1896; Pithead Lane, 27,300; head coach Corin Gilstead): Founded by salt miners; the club song is still sung before every home game.
- **Stonewick Rangers** (The Quarrymen, Stonewick, founded 1893; Quarry Lane, 24,600; head coach Raleigh Lynden): Promoted last season; a granite-quarry town whose fans carry chisels painted in club colours.
- **Tarnhaven Rovers** (The Tarns, Tarnhaven, founded 1891; Lakeside Ground, 23,300; head coach Marlow Linbury): A lake-district club with slate-grey shirts and a red sash.
- **Westmere Harriers** (The Larks, Westmere, founded 1903; Larkfield, 26,600; head coach Cuthbert Presworth): A club that began as an athletics society; its players still train on the old running track.
- **Wexmouth Town** (The Gulls, Wexmouth, founded 1895; Gull Point, 25,900; head coach Conrad Heathbrook): A seaside club with sky-blue shirts and a long cup tradition.
