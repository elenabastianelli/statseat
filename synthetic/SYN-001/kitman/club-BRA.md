# Brackwater Athletic

## Club profile

- Nickname: The Lamplighters. Founded in 1889 in Brackwater.
- Colours: navy blue and amber. Home kit: Navy shirt and navy shorts with amber socks; amber trim on the collar and cuffs. Away kit: Amber shirt with navy shorts and amber socks.
- Crest: A navy shield with an amber street lamp at its centre and a harbour chain across the bottom. Four small chimneys above the shield recall the Harrow Quay lamp factory, and a ribbon below carries the motto 'Lux in Portu' (light in the harbour).
- Stadium: Lanternworks Stadium (60,400 seats).
- Chairman: Ambrose Tallowfield, since 2015. Owner of a local ferry and shipping company and a Foundry End season-ticket holder for forty years. In 2015 he led the supporters' trust that bought the club back from outside owners, and he has chaired it ever since.
- Competition: Meridian League. Last season: 3rd. Now: 1st after 8 games, 20 points.
- Main rival: Calderholm United (The Estuary Derby).
- Club captain: Siebe Meulenkamp (#6). Vice-captain: Oberon Cotterell (#4).
- Formation: 4-2-3-1. Playing style: Possession-based, with quick switches of play to the wingers and full backs who push high.

## History

Brackwater Athletic was founded in 1889 by workers of the Harrow Quay lamp factory, who played on a cinder pitch next to the docks. The club took its nickname, The Lamplighters, from the factory's night shift, who lit the street lamps on the way to evening matches.

The club turned professional in 1904 and won its first league title in 1932. Its golden era came between 1978 and 1986, with three league titles and two Meridian Cups under head coach Walter Penhallow, whose statue stands outside the North stand.

In 2004 the club left its historic ground at Cinder Lane and moved to the Lanternworks Stadium, built on the site of the old lamp factory. The academy, opened the same year, has produced more than forty first-team players.

After a difficult decade, Brackwater finished third last season, their best league finish since 2002.

## Club legends

- Walter Penhallow, head coach 1976-1989: Led the club to three league titles; the statue outside the North stand shows him holding his famous flat cap.

- Desmond Ockley, goalkeeper 1995-2011: Played 512 league games, more than anyone in club history, and kept goal in the 2001/02 title season.

## Traditions

- Before kick-off the North stand holds up thousands of small amber lights, a nod to the lamp factory.
- The club anthem, 'Light the Quay', is sung as the teams walk out.
- A lamp is lit in the club museum after every home win and stays on until the next match.
