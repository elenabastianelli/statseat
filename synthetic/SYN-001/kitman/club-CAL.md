# Calderholm United

## Club profile

- Nickname: The Kilnmen. Founded in 1894 in Calderholm.
- Colours: maroon and sky blue. Home kit: Maroon shirt with white shorts and maroon socks. Away kit: Sky-blue shirt with maroon shorts and sky-blue socks.
- Crest: A maroon roundel with a white bottle kiln at its centre and two crossed potter's paddles beneath it, inside a sky-blue ring that reads 'Calderholm United 1894'.
- Stadium: The Kilnyard (38,200 seats).
- Chairman: Henrietta Glazebrook, since 2019. Runs a ceramics firm founded in the town's kiln district and has chaired the club since 2019. She paid for the restoration of the two brick kilns behind the South terrace, now the club museum.
- Competition: Meridian League. Last season: 5th. Now: 4th after 8 games, 15 points.
- Main rival: Brackwater Athletic (The Estuary Derby).
- Club captain: Mortimer Brightwell (#9). Vice-captain: Gijs Veenstra-Bakker (#11).
- Formation: 4-4-2. Playing style: Direct and intense: a high press, early balls to two strikers and many crosses from wide.

## History

Calderholm United was formed in 1894 by merging two works teams from the town's pottery kilns, which is why fans still call the players The Kilnmen.

The club won its first league title in 1952 and added three more, the last in 2015/16, when a squad built on a ferocious press lost only two games all season.

Its ground, The Kilnyard, keeps two of the original brick kilns behind the South terrace, now used as the club museum.

Calderholm finished fifth last season, Dario Castellane's third in charge.

## Club legends

- Agnes Thorley, club president 1948-1971: Saved the club from bankruptcy in 1949 by selling her pottery business.
- Fergal Mulvane, centre back 1988-2003: Captained the 1997 title team and never received a red card.
- Kojo Abrefa, forward 2010-2018: Scored the goal that won the 2016 title, a header in the 93rd minute, and is the club's record scorer.

## Traditions

- Away fans travel to Brackwater on 'the derby boat', the old estuary ferry.
- The Kilnyard crowd chants the minute of every Calderholm goal at the next home game.
- Players touch the brick of the old kiln on the way to the pitch.
