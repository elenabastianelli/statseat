## The Estuary Derby: head-to-head

Brackwater and Calderholm face each other across the Calder estuary, less than nine kilometres apart. The first meeting, in 1896, was abandoned at half-time when the tide flooded the pitch. Since then the derby has been played 212 times in all competitions, and the old ferry that links the two towns is still known as 'the derby boat'.
