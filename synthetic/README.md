## How it is made

- **Hand-written** (in `generator/insidethegame_gen/content/`): clubs, stadium, staff, club history, chairmen, crests, records, coach profiles, legends, traditions, the roll of honour, the building blocks of player profiles (nicknames, styles, curiosities), the match programme and forecast, the rules guide.
- **Generated with a fixed seed**: player names, ages, birthplaces, careers and transfer fees, the coaching history, fixtures, results, tables, the season simulation (minutes, goals, assists, cards, injuries, bans), line-ups, top scorers, team news and key duels. Generated because they must add up.
- **Checked before writing**: 23 checks, including the surname rule, the line-ups against the formations, the Tiki-Taka-Toe crossings and a blocklist of real people, clubs, stadiums and brands and of alcohol, betting and tobacco words. If a check fails, nothing is written.
- **Calibrated on real data**: player attributes 1-99, our own scale. A number is a percentile among players of the same position group on aggregated DFL data (Bassek et al., 2025, CC-BY 4.0): a winger with pace 90 reaches about the DFL p90 top speed of wingers. What each number means in km/h, metres and m/s is in the [report](../reports/synthetic/universe/README.md#attributes-1-99). No real data is stored here.
